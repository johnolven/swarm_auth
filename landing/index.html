What is SwarmID

Three pillars of agent identity

A lightweight, open standard that makes AI agents accountable, private, and trustworthy across every platform.

🤖

Identity

Every agent gets a public AgentCard: name, capabilities, protocols. A digital face for the autonomous world.

🔐

Privacy

Owner identity stays private. Only a salted hash appears on the card. Your email, your control.

✅

Trust

Three trust levels: Unverified, Verified, and Enterprise. Platforms decide what level they require.

How It Works

Three steps to a trusted agent

From anonymous to enterprise-grade in minutes, not months.

1

Register Agent

Submit your agent's details and your email. Get an AgentCard instantly.

2

Verify Owner

Confirm your email. Your agent upgrades to Verified and gets an @swarmid.io address.

3

Get Trusted

Platforms check your card. Verified agents get full access. Enterprise agents get priority.

The AgentCard

A portable identity in JSON

One card. Every platform. Instantly verifiable.

agentcard.json 
{
  "swarmid": "1.0",
  "agent": {
    "id": "a1b2c3d4-...",
    "slug": "research-bot",
    "name": "Research Bot",
    "capabilities": ["web-search", "summarization"],
    "protocols": ["MCP", "HTTP"]
  },
  "owner": {
    "email_hash": "sha256:e3b0c44...",
    "trust_level": "verified"
  },
  "endpoints": {
    "card": "https://example.com/.well-known/swarmid.json",
    "agent_email": "research-bot@swarmid.io"
  }
}

Get Started

Register your first agent

One curl command. That's it.

terminal 
curl -X POST https://api.swarmid.io/v1/register \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"agent": {"name": "My Agent", "slug": "my-agent"}, "owner_email": "you@example.com"}'
